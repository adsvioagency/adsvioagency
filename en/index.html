Questions

The things people ask before they book.

What if I already have a website?

We audit what you have first. Sometimes the right call is a rebuild. Sometimes it’s fixing the three things holding it back. If your current site is worth keeping, we’ll tell you — you pay less, and we spend the time on your profiles and content instead.

What exactly is a Visibility Scorecard?

A one-page document with your business scored out of 100 across five areas: your website, your search visibility, your social presence, your reviews, and the path a customer takes to reach you. It names the three things costing you the most right now, in plain language. You get it within 48 hours of the call and it’s yours to keep.

Is the score just a sales tactic?

It’s how we decide what to quote you, so it has to be accurate. If your website is fine and only your Google listing is broken, the score says that — and we quote you for the Google listing, not a rebuild. Being wrong about your score would cost us more than it gains us.

What happens after the 45 days?

Everything is yours. The site, the profiles, the content, the logins. No strings.

Most clients ask us to keep running the social and content side monthly. That’s optional, month to month, and we talk about it after launch — not before.

Do you work with businesses outside the US and the DR?

Yes. We work in English, French, Spanish and Kreyòl, and our clients are across the US, Canada and the Dominican Republic. Everything runs over video calls and WhatsApp, so what matters is whether the time zones line up. They usually do.

What do I actually have to do?

Less than you think. One kickoff call, one short review each week, and answers when we have questions. We write the copy, build the site, set up the profiles and produce your first month of content. Budget about two hours a week.

Why 45 days and not faster?

We could rush it. We’d rather get it right. Forty-five days gives us room to write proper copy, build properly, and let you review each stage without pressure. Most builds finish early — but we’d rather beat a realistic date than miss an optimistic one.

What if I need changes after launch?

Small changes in the first 30 days after launch are included — wording, hours, a photo, a price. Anything larger is quoted separately, or covered if you’re on a monthly plan. We don’t charge you to fix our own mistakes.

How do payments work?

Half to start, half on launch day. If that’s tight, there’s a three-month payment plan for a small convenience fee. No subscription to cancel, no auto-renewal, no lock-in.
