How the key is kept safe. Nothing on this page, and nothing in /assets/js/image-lab.js, ever sees GEMINI_API_KEY. The browser posts only your prompt to /.netlify/functions/generate-image. That function runs on Netlify's servers, reads the key from the environment, calls Gemini, and returns just the image. Open devtools and read the network tab if you want to confirm it: the key is not in the request, the response, or the page source.
