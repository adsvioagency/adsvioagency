Concept build

Atelier Nord is a fictional hair studio and spa in Santo Domingo, built by Adsvio to show what a Storefront Launch ships when the customer thinks in Spanish. It is not a real business and not a client project.

See what this costs

The decision

Spanish leads and English never does. Half of the salons in this market run an English-first site because the template came that way, and it quietly tells local customers the place is not for them.

Why it looks like this

Fully rounded shapes, a chunky geometric face and colour at full strength. A salon sells how something feels, so the page is allowed to be loud, the exact opposite of the restraint on the Clarité build.

What it has to do

End in a WhatsApp message. Not a form, not a booking engine with an account. Prices are published so the message that arrives is “Tuesday at 3?” and not “how much?”
