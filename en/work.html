Why they look nothing alike

A template would have been faster.

A restaurant, a consultant and a salon are selling three different things to three different people. If all three sites looked the same, at least two of them would be wrong. Same craft, same 45 days, three different voices — that is the whole point being made.

What is the same

The process, the deliverables, the 45-day guarantee, and the standard of the build: fast, accessible, mobile-first, and set up to be found.

What changes

Typography, colour, imagery, the order of the page, and which action the whole thing is pushing toward — a table, a call, or a WhatsApp message.

What you get

A site that looks like your business rather than like our last client’s. Your competitors are using the same three templates as each other.
