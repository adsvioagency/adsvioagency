Google Business Profile setup and optimization.

For a local business this is the highest-return hour of work available anywhere in marketing. It is also the thing almost everyone half-finishes and then forgets about.

Why this matters more than your website

When someone searches “hair salon near me” or “Haitian food Miami”, the first thing they see is not a website. It is a map with three businesses on it, each with a rating, a photo, opening hours and a Call button.

Most people choose from that map without visiting anyone’s website at all. Which means your Google Business Profile is not a supporting act. For a local business, it is the storefront.

And it is free. That is the part that catches people out — because it is free, it gets treated as unimportant, and a business that would happily spend $2,000 on a website leaves its profile with no categories, four photos from 2019 and last year’s hours.

What “optimized” actually means

Not much mystery here. Google ranks local results on three things: relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot change your distance. The other two are almost entirely within your control and almost always neglected.

Categories, which most people get wrong

Your primary category carries more weight than nearly anything else, and secondary categories decide which searches you can appear in at all. A salon that also does nails and lashes but has only picked “Hair salon” is invisible for two of the three things it sells.

Completeness, which Google measures

Services with descriptions and prices. Attributes. The full opening hours including holidays. A description written for people rather than stuffed with keywords. Products. The booking link. Google rewards a complete profile because a complete profile makes for a better result, and most of your competitors have not filled theirs in.

Photos, which do more than you think

Profiles with recent photos get noticeably more clicks and direction requests. Not stock photos — the actual room, the actual food, the actual people. Ten good ones beat forty bad ones, and they need refreshing rather than uploading once.

Reviews, which are the whole ballgame

Volume, recency, rating and whether you reply. Most businesses have no system for asking, so they get reviews by accident and mostly from people who were annoyed. That is a fixable problem, and fixing it is worth more than any other single thing on this page.

Questions

Questions about the profile.

I already have a profile. Is that enough?

Having one and having a working one are different things. In our audits, the average existing profile is about a third complete.

Usually the categories are wrong, the services are empty, the photos are old and there is no review system. All of that is fixable in days.

What if I do not have a physical address?

You can still have a profile as a service-area business. You define the areas you serve and hide the street address, which is the correct setup for consultants, trades and anyone working from home.

Verification is sometimes fussier. We handle that part.

How long until I see a difference?

Profile changes often show within days, and the completeness work tends to move things fastest. Category changes take a few weeks to settle.

Reviews are the slow compounding part — that is a quarter, not a week. Anyone promising you the top of the map in a fortnight is guessing.

Can you get me to the top of the map?

No, and neither can anyone else honestly. Distance from the searcher is a ranking factor nobody controls, so a competitor two blocks closer may always beat you for some searches.

What we can do is make sure you win every comparison where you are genuinely the better choice, which is where most businesses are losing right now.
