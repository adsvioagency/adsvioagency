The short version

We collect what you type into our forms, and — only if you accept cookies — anonymous data about which pages get read. We never sell anything to anyone. You can have it all deleted by asking, and we will do it within 45 days.

Who we are

This site is operated by Adsvio Agency, a sole proprietorship registered in the Dominican Republic. For data protection purposes, Pierre Runald Février is the data controller.

Where your data goes

We are based in the Dominican Republic. Our host and the services above operate servers in the United States and the European Union. If you are in the EU or the UK, this means your data may be transferred outside your region.

The providers we use rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision for those transfers. We have chosen providers that do, rather than the cheapest available.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that did not become work — 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client records — 7 years after the last invoice, because tax authorities require it.
  • Checklist and newsletter subscribers — until you unsubscribe, then removed within 30 days.
  • Analytics — 14 months, which is Google Analytics' shortest retention setting, and we use it.
  • Server logs — as long as our host keeps them, typically 30 days.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to do all of the following, and we will, free of charge, within 45 days:

  • See it. A copy of everything we hold about you.
  • Correct it. Fix anything wrong.
  • Delete it. Remove it entirely, unless we are legally required to keep it.
  • Take it elsewhere. A machine-readable copy you can hand to somebody else.
  • Stop us. Object to a use, or withdraw consent you previously gave.

If you are in the EU or the UK, these are your rights under the GDPR, and our lawful bases are consent (for analytics and marketing email), contract (for delivering work you have engaged us for) and legitimate interest (for replying to an enquiry you sent us). You also have the right to complain to your national supervisory authority.

If you are in Quebec, Law 25 gives you equivalent rights, including the right to know when a decision about you was made by automated means. We do not make automated decisions about anyone.

To exercise any of this, use the contact form and say what you want. You do not have to explain why.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS with HSTS. Form submissions are encrypted in transit. Access to enquiry data is limited to Pierre and any specialist actively working on your project, and those specialists are under confidentiality terms.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data and creates a real risk to you, we will tell you and the relevant authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

Children

This site is for business owners. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we will update the date at the top of this page and, if the change affects how we use data you already gave us, ask for your consent again through the banner.
