## Current content audit

Strengths: recognisable design, plain-language pages, server-delivered text, clear primary CTA, useful service/guide clusters, disclosed concept work, distinct call/text and WhatsApp numbers.

Gaps: homepage does not plainly name the service mix early; service pages repeat package assumptions and a 30-minute audit invitation rather than consistently explaining the Scorecard journey; social media copy has little professional/trades specificity; local SEO and GBP copy overstates ranking control; some articles assert unsupported ratios, timings and returns. Haitian page confuses founder origin with company founding location. Audience coverage skews toward storefronts. No clear South Florida remote-service page. Resource navigation and contextual links can better connect buyer problems to services. Cost guidance presents unsupported market rates as universal truth. Several visible FAQ answers need correction together with their JSON-LD.

Keep the homepage H1/animated language treatment, offer/qualifier mechanics, portfolio/concept pages, About biography, all French pages, legal pages, navigation and visual components. No new Spanish/Kreyol URLs or fabricated translations. No local office or regulated-industry expertise claims. Do not change tracking identifiers without actual accounts.

## Cannibalization and new-page decisions

Service pages own WHAT Adsvio does; audience pages own HOW it applies to a buyer; regional page owns WHERE remote delivery fits; guides own HOW TO/WHEN/COST; articles own a narrower question. Website-cost guide must not duplicate the service sales pitch. Local SEO owns the wider site/listings system; GBP page owns profile delivery; troubleshooting stays in its article. Coaches remains focused on the individual expert and consultation journey; professional services covers practices/firms and approval-sensitive content. Local-business page remains the broad storefront context while home-services covers traveling teams and quote requests.

Add only three substantive pages: professional services, home services, South Florida. Do not add separate Miami/Fort Lauderdale/West Palm Beach, law/accounting/medical/roofer/HVAC, Instagram/Facebook pages now. Their evidence and unique content are not yet sufficient. No new generic AI-SEO service promise. Explain search accessibility on the existing local-SEO page. No llms.txt ranking claims or special AI markup.

## Metadata, schema and conversion rules

Use a unique descriptive service/audience title plus Adsvio, a factual invitation in the description, and the same Open Graph/Twitter text. Preserve established URL/canonical shape. Keep organization identity at /#organization and website at /#website. New pages get WebPage/BreadcrumbList and only visible matching FAQ data. No ratings, reviews or offices in schema. Updated article dates reflect actual edits, not a sitewide freshness reset. Keep author identity and datePublished.

Answers should lead with the useful response, followed by actionable examples. Connect the five Score dimensions (website, search, social, trust, customer journey) to the appropriate service. Do not add extra forms or funnel steps. New links are contextual within existing styled components. No CSS/framework/dependency additions.

## Validation and measurement

Run existing QA, partial consistency and repository SEO checks; inspect changed/new pages at mobile/desktop sizes, navigation, FAQ interaction and existing form validation without sending live leads. Validate fragment links, JSON-LD against visible FAQs, sitemap membership, canonicals and deliberate noindex. Review full diff and protect internal docs. Static site has no build step.

After owner approves publishing: inspect priority pages in Search Console; compare Google-selected canonicals and URL exclusions; submit sitemap in Bing Webmaster Tools; confirm eligible business-profile details. Configure actual GA4 consent-aware measurement only after account/property IDs exist. Treat click/qualifier events as steps, not booked calls. Review nonbranded impressions, clicks, page/query relevance and qualified organic leads over 28-day periods; annotate launch and allow time for recrawling. AI referrals are partially observable; no guaranteed citations or model-answer ranking report. See the separate keyword tracking list for groups and priority.
