The decision

Nobody hires a consultant from a website. They decide whether to book a call, so the whole page is built to survive a sceptical ten-minute read and end in one click.

Why it looks like this

Restraint is the product. A serif set at a long measure, four-fifths of the page left empty, and one accent colour used four times. Judgement is sold by what you leave out.

What it has to do

Catch the two-thirds who are not ready yet. The guide in the middle of the page is the second-best outcome, and it is the one most visitors take.

What I do

Four engagements, in the order most clients need them.

Most people arrive at number two, in a hurry. It usually turns out they needed number one eighteen months ago.

  1. 01 Policy & handbook review What your documents actually say, what Quebec law requires them to say, and the gap between the two. Delivered as a marked-up document, not a report you will not read.
  2. 02 Investigations Harassment and misconduct complaints, handled by someone outside your organization. Documented to a standard that holds up if it ever has to.
  3. 03 Restructuring & terminations Planning the sequence, writing the scripts, sitting in the room if you want me there. The goal is that people leave with their dignity and you leave without a claim.
  4. 04 Manager training Half-day sessions for the people who have the conversations. In French, English, or both in the same room, which is usually what Montreal actually needs.
