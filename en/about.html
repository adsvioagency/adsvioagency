The founder

From Pétion-Ville to Santo Domingo — and to your business.

It started with one question.

Why aren’t we online?

I’m from Pétion-Ville, Haiti, and I live in Santo Domingo. For five years I worked with international clients as a virtual assistant and project manager.

In those five years I noticed something I couldn’t unsee. The most talented entrepreneurs I knew — my friends, my family, people in my community — were running real businesses with real expertise. Online, most of them were nearly invisible.

Meanwhile I was helping clients in the US and Canada build clean brands and clean systems. They had the budget, the team, the polish. Our entrepreneurs back home had the talent. They just didn’t have the support.

Our people are talented. Our culture is rich. Our businesses deserve to look the part.

That gap — between what our people deserve and what they actually have — is why Adsvio exists.

Adsvio isn’t just an agency. It’s a bridge. We take the same strategy, design and execution the big international agencies sell, and we bring it to Caribbean and diaspora businesses at a price that makes sense for a business that is still growing.

We speak your language. Literally — English, French, Spanish, Kreyòl. We understand your culture because it’s ours. And we take your work as seriously as you do, because we know what it takes to build something from nothing.

If you’re tired of looking at your business and wishing the online side matched the real value you bring, you’re in the right place.
