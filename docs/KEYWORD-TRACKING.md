# Search-query tracking list

Prepared 29 September 2026. This is a prioritized monitoring list, not a ranking report. There are no claimed search volumes, positions or difficulty scores. P1 means high business relevance and a useful existing destination; P2 means a supporting topic to evaluate; P3 means exploratory or a later content opportunity. Priority does not imply likely ranking speed.

## How to use the list

After an approved release, record the publication date. In Google Search Console, review the actual queries and destination pages under Performance, comparing 28-day periods and allowing for recrawling. Separate Adsvio brand variations from service/problem searches. Inspect impressions, clicks, click-through rate and average position together; the average is not a fixed rank. Save the baseline exports. Sparse or omitted queries are not proof of zero demand.

Record: period, query or query group, page, country/device where useful, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, qualified inquiries and notes. Query phrases containing a city indicate wording, not verified searcher residence. Search Console does not give a complete city-level customer report. Start with P1 groups and expand where impressions or actual inquiries show relevance.

## AI referrals and manual observations

Once a real GA4 property is configured with consent and validated events, inspect observed referral/source data for actual AI-search visits. Keep source, landing page, engaged visits and qualified inquiries separate. Referrers may be missing; direct traffic does not prove an AI source. Google AI-feature traffic is not a complete cross-platform citation report.

For a small manual check, record the exact prompt, platform, date, response and cited URL. Example prompts: “What should a small professional practice improve before paying for ads?” or “Who can help a South Florida contractor with website and social content remotely?” Record whether Adsvio is cited without presenting one answer as a durable ranking. Do not manufacture testimonials, repeated mentions or fake comparisons to influence answers.

## Review decisions

- If two pages repeatedly compete for the same query and neither matches intent well, clarify their roles before adding content.
- If an industry group attracts qualified inquiries, collect permissioned questions and examples before considering a dedicated industry page.
- If impressions grow without useful clicks, inspect title/description relevance and the result context.
- If clicks grow without qualified Score requests, inspect the service explanation and contact journey before publishing more articles.
- Keep actual bookings, qualified conversations and signed work separate from button clicks and completed qualifier forms.
