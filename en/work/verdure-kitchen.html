Verdure Kitchen is a fictional Caribbean restaurant in Miami, built by Adsvio to show what a Storefront Launch ships. It is not a real business and not a client project.

See what this costs

The decision

People choose a restaurant while hungry, on a phone, within a few blocks. So the page leads with food and keeps hours, address and the reserve button one thumb away at every scroll position.

Why it looks like this

Dark ground so the food is the brightest thing on screen. A soft serif with real warmth instead of a restaurant-template script. Prices set in plain figures, because hiding them loses more covers than it protects.

What it has to do

Turn a search for “Haitian food near me” into a booked table. Everything else on the page is in service of that one action.
