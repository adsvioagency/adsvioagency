Website design

Small business website design that does one job well.

A referral checks your website before calling. A homeowner wants to know whether you cover their area. A prospective client needs a reason to trust you. Your website should answer those questions and make contacting you easy.

The short version

We plan, write and build small business websites around the decisions your customers need to make. The free Visibility Score comes first. You receive priorities, then an agreed scope and fixed quote for the work your business needs.

What a useful business website needs to answer

What do you do, who is it for, and what should someone do next? Those answers belong near the top of the page. Clear service information, genuine proof and a working contact route help a visitor decide whether to inquire.

For a practice or professional firm

A law office, accountant or consultant needs to explain its work without making the reader decode industry language. Service pages, accurate team information and a clear consultation process can make a referral easier to act on. See our approach for professional services.

For a contractor or home service business

A homeowner needs your service area, the type of work you accept and a straightforward way to request an estimate. Real project photos and an explanation of what happens after the inquiry help remove uncertainty. Read the home services approach.

Should you repair your website or rebuild it?

Keep a website that works. If the layout is sound, clearer copy, a repaired form or better service pages may be enough. A rebuild makes more sense when mobile use, maintenance or the structure gets in the way of essential improvements.

We review the existing pages and contact journey before recommending either. We also identify old URLs that need redirects, useful content worth retaining and connections that must survive the change.

Design follows the message

We agree on the audience, services and next action before writing and designing. Then we connect the site to the relevant profiles, booking or contact tools and check the journey on a phone. Scope and integrations are agreed before work starts.

For the budgeting questions, use our small business website cost guide. If social is currently your only online presence, read when a website helps alongside Instagram.

Questions

Questions about the website itself.

How many pages do I need?

Enough to explain your services clearly and give visitors a useful next step. We agree the page list after the audit. A new service page should answer a distinct customer question, rather than repeat another page for a different keyword.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, hosting account and logins belong to you. Your scope explains handover, ongoing costs and any third-party tools, so you know what you are responsible for after launch.

Can I update it myself?

We agree which updates you need to make, such as hours, prices or photos, and cover the process at handover. Larger structural changes and ongoing support are discussed separately.

What if I already have a website?

We audit it first. If the existing site is worth keeping, the recommendation may be better copy, repaired contact paths or a few useful pages. A redesign is recommended only when the current structure gets in the way.
